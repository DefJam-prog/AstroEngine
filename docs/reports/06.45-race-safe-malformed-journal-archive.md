# 06.45 — Preserve malformed campaign journal during archival

## Scope and decision

Assessed the confirmed archive flow introduced in 06.44. Its last path-based reread and later `std::filesystem::remove(path)` were separated by a TOCTOU window: another process could replace the journal after validation but before removal. Standard C++ filesystem does not provide an atomic compare-and-delete primitive, and platform locking/deletion semantics differ. This substage removes source retirement from the malformed-journal archive flow rather than claiming a portable lock.

## Changes

- Runtime still revalidates the displayed bounded `Malformed` record, reads its exact bytes, creates a fingerprint-named adjacent archive exclusively and verifies its contents. It no longer removes or mutates the source journal.
- A matching existing archive is an idempotent retry. A conflicting/unsafe archive is preserved and reported; it is never overwritten.
- Editor labels and confirmation now say “create archive copy”. Success reports that the original remains. Smoke verifies exact bytes in both files and confirms the candidate slot-looking save is unchanged. The pending malformed journal continues blocking transfer/resume until manual review.
- Integration tests cover explicit confirmation, exact-copy preservation, idempotent repeat, archive collision and stale source after review.
- Updated README, architecture/roadmap/status/decisions/testing/save-system documentation. D107 records the TOCTOU finding and limits.

## Limits and next work

No cross-process stress test or Windows/POSIX lock implementation was added. Exact-byte fingerprints are non-cryptographic. The source journal remains a blocker by design. The separate acknowledgement operation for valid journals has its own deletion policy and was not changed here. Recommended next substage: 06.46, assess and test race-safe retirement semantics for valid recovery-journal acknowledgement across the supported Windows target and future POSIX targets before changing that operation.
